## Intent Alignment
- Source: Gitea issue #32 supplied verbatim by the human; remediation order in master issue #40; automated continuation and focused-validation override supplied 2026-07-30
- Understanding: Whitespace-only manual Cleanup edits currently preserve roles by list index whenever the paragraph count is unchanged. A merge before an existing scene separator plus a split after it shifts content while preserving count, allowing the old scene_separator role to land on prose. Both deterministic and AI Chunking treat that explicit role as authoritative and omit the prose without warning. Replace index alignment with unambiguous normalized-content matching, preserve classifier-positive scene separators, and fall back to classifier-only roles when content cannot be matched safely.
- Risk: `low`
- Decision: `proceed` - The failure and downstream consequence are reproduced by a narrow persisted-role branch, the content-match policy is deterministic and conservative, existing requirements already bound the contract, and focused end-to-end coverage can prove no prose is lost without changing Chunking semantics.

Out of scope: lexical-edit behavior, scene-separator classifier rules, deterministic or AI Chunking implementation, starts_after_scene_separator issue #35, general paragraph diffing, UI changes, and the full pytest suite explicitly deferred by the human due runtime.
