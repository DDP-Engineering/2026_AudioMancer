
QWEN TTS VOICE DESIGNER GUIDE
=============================

INTRODUCTION
------------
Qwen3-TTS Voice Designer works best with highly descriptive prompts that define:
- vocal timbre
- age
- pacing
- emotional tone
- articulation
- personality
- delivery style
- cinematic context

The model responds much better to acoustic and emotional descriptors than to simple character labels.

GOOD:
"A mature male voice with deep resonance, calm pacing, and restrained emotional authority."

BAD:
"An old king voice."

------------------------------------------------------------
1. IDEAL PROMPT LENGTH
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended:
80–220 words

Best practical range:
120–180 words

Too short:
- voice becomes generic
- unstable emotional identity
- inconsistent speech rhythm

Too long:
- conflicting instructions
- overfitting to irrelevant details
- unstable tone generation

The best prompts are:
- dense
- descriptive
- focused
- cinematic
- acoustically grounded

------------------------------------------------------------
2. BEST PROMPT STRUCTURE
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended structure:

1. Age + gender
2. Vocal timbre
3. Speaking rhythm
4. Emotional profile
5. Delivery style
6. Personality cues
7. Optional cinematic context

Example structure:

"A mature female voice with a low resonant register and calm deliberate pacing.
Speaks with controlled intelligence and emotional restraint.
Clear articulation with subtle warmth beneath a stern exterior.
Elegant and authoritative delivery suitable for a political leader in a cinematic science fiction audiobook."

------------------------------------------------------------
3. IMPORTANT ACOUSTIC DESCRIPTORS
------------------------------------------------------------

Useful terms:

Timbre:
- warm
- deep
- resonant
- gravelly
- smooth
- breathy
- sharp
- velvety
- textured
- airy
- metallic
- soft
- rough

Pacing:
- deliberate
- measured
- calm
- rapid
- controlled
- fluid
- rhythmic
- clipped
- reflective

Emotional style:
- restrained
- melancholic
- intelligent
- intense
- confident
- mysterious
- intimidating
- compassionate
- playful
- detached

Articulation:
- crisp
- precise
- refined
- elegant
- natural
- conversational

------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT WORKS BEST
------------------------------------------------------------

Qwen performs especially well with:
- cinematic narration styles
- emotionally restrained voices
- audiobook-style performances
- subtle emotional complexity
- realistic human delivery

The model is strongest when:
- emotions are implied instead of exaggerated
- pacing is described
- voice texture is specified
- the character has psychological depth

------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT TO AVOID
------------------------------------------------------------

Avoid:
- excessive lore
- plot summaries
- visual descriptions only
- contradictory descriptors
- meme language
- overly theatrical wording

BAD:
"A gigantic warrior with blue eyes and golden armor from planet X."

GOOD:
"A powerful male voice with deep resonance and calm disciplined authority."

Avoid conflicting instructions:

BAD:
"Soft and aggressive."
"Emotionless but highly emotional."
"Fast slow pacing."

------------------------------------------------------------
6. CHARACTER DESIGN BEST PRACTICES
------------------------------------------------------------

Instead of describing appearance:
Describe how the character SOUNDS.

Instead of:
"Beautiful young princess"

Use:
"A refined young female voice with graceful pacing and educated diction."

Instead of:
"Strong warrior"

Use:
"A rough textured male voice with deliberate pacing and natural command."

Think in terms of:
- microphone performance
- acting direction
- audiobook narration
- vocal psychology

------------------------------------------------------------
7. AUDIOBOOK BEST PRACTICES
------------------------------------------------------------

For audiobook projects:

1. Keep narrator voices neutral and stable
2. Keep emotional range controlled
3. Use subtle differentiation between characters
4. Avoid cartoon exaggeration
5. Maintain consistent pacing descriptors

Recommended narrator traits:
- smooth
- cinematic
- clear articulation
- emotionally controlled
- natural rhythm

------------------------------------------------------------
8. VOICE CONSISTENCY
------------------------------------------------------------

For best consistency:
- generate one stable reference voice
- reuse the same prompt
- modify only small emotional elements

Do NOT completely rewrite prompts between chapters.

Stable prompts produce:
- better continuity
- better pronunciation consistency
- more stable emotional identity

------------------------------------------------------------
9. ADVANCED TECHNIQUES
------------------------------------------------------------

Layering technique:
Use 3 levels:

1. Acoustic layer
2. Emotional layer
3. Character layer

Example:

Acoustic:
"Deep resonant male voice"

Emotional:
"Restrained melancholy"

Character:
"A leader carrying immense responsibility"

Combined:
"A deep resonant male voice with restrained melancholy and calm authoritative delivery suitable for a leader burdened by prophecy."

------------------------------------------------------------
10. GOOD TEMPLATE
------------------------------------------------------------

TEMPLATE:

"A [age] [gender] voice with [timbre].
Speaks with [pacing] and [articulation].
Carries [emotional profile].
Delivery is [style].
Suitable for [character archetype/context]."

------------------------------------------------------------
11. EXAMPLE HIGH-QUALITY PROMPT
------------------------------------------------------------

"A mature male voice with deep resonance and calm measured pacing.
Clear articulate diction with restrained emotional intensity.
Speaks with intelligence, quiet authority, and subtle melancholy.
Smooth cinematic delivery with natural conversational rhythm.
Suitable for a philosopher-king in a dramatic science fiction audiobook."

------------------------------------------------------------
12. FINAL RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Best overall strategy:
- Think like a voice director
- Focus on sound, not visuals
- Keep prompts emotionally coherent
- Use cinematic realism
- Prefer subtlety over exaggeration
- Use stable prompt structures
- Reuse reference voices whenever possible

The best Qwen TTS voices usually feel:
- human
- grounded
- emotionally believable
- restrained
- cinematic
